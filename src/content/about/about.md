### Origin

I got into software through curiosity — a secondhand machine, free tutorials, and a lot of broken projects that eventually ran. That loop of breaking things and fixing them stuck.

### Journey

Over the past few years I've built full-stack products in the energy sector, led a core migration from a legacy stack to a modern one (Lighthouse 60 → 100), and cut a query from 90 seconds to under one. I've led small teams, reviewed code, and learned that the hardest part of any project is usually not the code.

### Current

I'm focused on integrating AI into developer workflows — not as a novelty, but as a way to remove the repetitive work so the team can concentrate on the hard problems. I work remotely for a Spanish energy company and keep my tools sharp for EU opportunities.
